## Findings

- **The TRIG key cycles two sub-pages, as on the Rytm** (Trig Parameters, then Euclidean). seedr can open as a third
  TRIG page there, as on the Rytm (SD3) [measured in analog-emu].
- **Power off keeps nothing in analog-emu.** `--power-off` raises the Rytm's power-sense interrupt; the Syntakt's
  handler runs and parks the card in 3.0M instructions, but writes 0 sectors, and after a cold boot the edit is gone.
  analog-emu models the Rytm's save (its pins and the raw slots 0x7a000 / 0x7c000). Not known: whether a Syntakt
  saves its working state at power off, where, and on which signal.
- `dtpeek` reads the firmware from `DTEMU_FW`; without it, it looks for the Digitakt's file.
