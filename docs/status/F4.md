## Decisions taken

1. **Lengths: the other track repeats** (step i reads TRK's step i mod its length), as both tracks play from the
   pattern's start. With a longer TRK, its first steps. The alternative (steps past TRK's length as empty) would
   make COPY from a 16-step track onto a 64-step one give one bar and three empty ones.
2. **COPY takes positions only** (design section 5: "the trig positions of another track"). The new trigs carry
   the track's defaults, not TRK's velocities, notes or locks. Copying whole steps would need the device to read
   another track's locks into the take (origins name steps of the track in scope only).
3. **A removed trig is SKIP's cleared step**: fields and locks back to the defaults, origin none. MASK and SKIP
   both remove, and both leave the same kind of step.
4. **MASK touches only steps with a trig.** A step without one is left as it is (its swing, its unknown bits).
5. **TRK / MTRK = the track in scope is allowed**, read from the original (the context holds the original for the
   track in scope, in `take()` and in the preview). COPY self restarts on the same rhythm; MASK self with ROT keeps
   (ONLY) or drops (AVOID) the rotated trigs that land on original ones.
6. **Out of range is refused** (`SEED_E_ARG`): TRK or MTRK outside the context's tracks, or an unknown MASK value.
   The page stops TRK and MTRK at the device's last track, so this happens only with a recipe written from outside.
7. **The context's trigs are taken as they are**: a muted trig counts as a trig; a track in Euclidean mode gives
   its stored trigs, not the pattern Euclidean mode plays (F2 found the flag words are not changed by it).

## Open points

- **The title bar shows the seed (`S2`) with SRC = COPY**, although COPY draws no random numbers (as F2's open point
  for EUCLID).
- **A Euclidean-mode track as TRK or MTRK** gives its stored trigs, not what it plays (decision 7). The device's
  context could read the pattern Euclidean mode plays (+0x2D1, F2's firmware finding) for such a track; that is the
  device layer's (`rt_seed_context`), so it is reported, not changed.
- **`props.h`'s random recipes** draw only RANDOM as a source and never turn MASK on, so `test_props.c` does not
  cover F4; `test_src_copy.c` and `test_place_mask.c` run the same checks with COPY and MASK forced (as F2 did for
  EUCLID). The lead may add both to `prop_layer`.
- **`10-pages.sh`'s "a source that is not built" check** uses MAP (F2's change). COPY is now built, so when F3 lands
  that check needs another unbuilt layer.
- **TRK + a TRIG key while seedr is open** selects another track: seedr stays open and RECORD with the TRIG keys
  records on that track (`24-copy-mask` relies on it). What seedr's screen shows while SD is selected is not
  checked.
