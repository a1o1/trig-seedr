## Decisions taken

1. **A letter is a 16-step phrase, so pages with the same letter repeat.** The PHRS loop in `seed_render.c` (0.1)
   rendered take n + offset(p) over the whole track and copied page p's steps out of it. With that, AAAA was the
   same as OFF and ABAB's pages 1 and 3 were two different parts of take n: no letter repeated. The design says
   "a 16-step phrase over the pages (AAAA, …; a letter is a take offset)". Page p is now rendered from a 16-step
   track holding the original's page p, as take n + offset(p), and copied back with its origins moved by 16 × p.
   The change is the body of `seed_render`'s PHRS branch and a 2-byte-word copy helper (`copy16`); `take()`, `best()`
   and `chain()` are unchanged. No contract change: the page's original lives in `w->page`, its take in `w->a`
   (CVAR then merges into `w->a`, decision 6).
2. **What belongs to the track's positions stays there.** The phrase's own: every random number (the slots are by
   step in the phrase), DENS's count, ROT, SYNC, the meter's ranks (over 16 steps). The track's: SKIP (the
   track's first N steps; the page is rendered with SKIP − 16 × p), the groove (SHFT − 16 × p, so a 32-step or
   7-step groove runs along the track), and the other tracks MASK and COPY read (the context is shifted per page).
   The alternative, everything phrase-local, would clear SKIP's steps on every page and restart a 32-step groove
   on each page.
3. **Shorter tracks** (A2: "page by page as far as the track goes"). A last page shorter than 16 steps plays the
   first steps of its letter's phrase; the phrase's steps past the track's end have no trigs (cleared, so
   SOURCE = TRACK cannot pick up steps outside the length). A track of 16 steps or fewer is one phrase: PHRS
   renders it as OFF does, byte for byte. Consequence: ROT or SYNC can move a trig into the hidden part of a last,
   short page, where it is not written.
4. **Origins past the track's end are none.** An empty step rotated in from the hidden part of a short page would
   otherwise name an original step past the length.
5. **The meter under PHRS:** ranks are over the 16-step phrase. A meter whose bar does not divide 16 (3/4 with
   BEAT 4 is 12 steps) restarts at each page. A repeated 16-step phrase and a 12-step bar cannot both line up, so
   this follows from "a 16-step phrase".
6. **CVAR merge.** A trig in both takes is take n's step as it is, its condition from the TRIG page included
   ("plays plain" read as: CVAR adds nothing). A trig only in take n is take n's step with CVAR's condition; one
   only in take n + 1 is take n + 1's whole step (fields, lanes, origin) with its condition. Steps without a trig
   and steps past the length are take n's. `seed_cvar_merge` accepts `out == a` (used by decision 1).
7. **AAAB.** The Rytm has 1:4 … 4:4 but no "not 4:4". Take n + 1's trigs get 4:4; take n's get !PRE (code 25),
   which plays when the last condition evaluated on the track was false. That holds only after a 4:4 trig in the
   same loop, so a take-n-only trig before the first 4:4 trig plays plain (in all four loops; the closest single
   condition: one loop too many). See open point 1.
8. **CVAR on a track without conditions** (`SEED_F_COND` off) is refused with `SEED_E_NA`; nothing is written.

## Findings

- **`seed_render.c`'s PHRS gave no repetition** (decision 1).
- **A struct copy compiles to `memcpy`** with m68k-elf-gcc 16.2 (`pr = *r` for the 180-byte recipe), which a mod
  does not link; `make -C lib coldfire` reported it. `copy16` (a 16-bit word loop) compiles without it.
- **Stack:** `seed_render` uses 472 bytes on ColdFire (`-fstack-usage`) for the page's recipe and context.

## Open points

1. **!PRE is not checked against the device.** AAAB relies on PRE / !PRE reading the last condition evaluated on
   the track *other than PRE / !PRE* (Elektron's description of PRE, as I read it). If stock counts PRE trigs too,
   two take-n-only trigs in a row after a 4:4 alternate instead. I tried to settle it in the emulator (trigs and
   conditions poked into A01 BD's record, PLAY, BD's voice from `--usb-wav`), but the poked conditions did not
   change what BD played, so the run says nothing either way. It needs a listen on the Rytm, or an emulator
   recipe for watching which trigs play; the hardware checklist should include it.
2. **For Alan / the lead:** decisions 1–3 (what a letter is, what stays on the track's positions, short tracks),
   6 (a trig in both keeps take n's step) and 7 (AAAB with !PRE, plain before the first 4:4).
3. **QUAL (F11) with CVAR and PHRS:** each of takes n and n + 1 (and each page) is scored on its own, as 0.1's
   `take()` does; the merged track is not scored.

## Requests to the lead
