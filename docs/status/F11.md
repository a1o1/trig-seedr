# F11: Candidate scoring (QUAL)

State: design note, for the lead's agreement before any code
Branch: worktree-f11-score

## The design proposed

`seed_render.c` `best()` renders candidates 0 .. QUAL − 1 of take n and keeps the one with the highest
`seed_score`. A tie goes to the lower candidate. F11 fills `seed_score` in `lib/score.c`.

**What a score measures.** The score reads only the trig positions of the rendered track and the recipe's RHYTHM,
PLACE and TAKE settings. It does not read velocity, notes, locks, conditions or timing. So no DYNAMICS..TIME knob
can change which candidate wins (I3 for QUAL). It has two terms, compared in order:

1. **Count kept (C).** RANDOM only: `C = 255 − min(255, |trigs − asked|)`, where asked = `seed_share(DENS, grid
   positions)`, the count the source gives (I5). Candidates differ in C only when MASK or SKIP removes trigs. Then
   QUAL chooses the take that keeps most of what DENS asked for. For other sources, C = 255: TRACK, EUCLID and COPY
   draw no rhythm, and MAP's count has no asked value.
2. **Fit to the meter (F).** The mean rank of the trigs, `seed_rank(METR, BEAT, ROLE, step, length)` (F1), 0..65535.
   For a negative BIAS the rank is reversed, as in `seed_keys`, so QUAL never pulls against BIAS. At BIAS 0, F
   favours ROLE's strong steps. No trigs: F = 0.

`score = C << 16 | F`, 0 .. 2^24 − 1. C decides first, and F decides when C is equal.

**What QUAL does, by source.** RANDOM with BIAS below ±100: it chooses among QUAL takes. BIAS ±100 gives the same
rhythm for every candidate. TRACK, EUCLID and COPY also give the same rhythm for every candidate (SYNC aside). All
candidates then tie, candidate 0 wins, and QUAL > 1 gives the same take as QUAL 1. MAP: candidates differ only
through CHAOS. Raising QUAL from q to q + 1 keeps the take unless the new candidate scores strictly higher.

**Cost.** The score is one pass over the track plus one `seed_rank` per trig (at most 64 per candidate). The renders
cost more: QUAL q is q chains. F10 measured 194,467 instructions (0.97 ms) for a 64-step render with most layers on.
QUAL 8 would be about 1.6 M instructions (8 ms) per preview render. `31-score.sh` measures this with F10's method.

**P3: a take stays a function of its seed number.** Candidate c of take n is a function of (n, c, recipe, original,
context), so the best of them is too. No history is stored, and PHRS and CVAR call `best()` per take as now.

**VARY (0.1's decision 9).** VARY relates candidate c of take n + 1 to candidate c of take n. It does not relate the
winners: when take n's winner is candidate 2 and take n + 1's is candidate 5, the two shown takes are independent.
VARY 0 and VARY 100 stay exact (unrelated takes; equal takes, the same winner). In between, the share kept between
shown takes is at least P(same winner) × VARY and at most VARY. High VARY changes scores little, so the same
candidate tends to win again. I10 is tested at QUAL 1; `test_score` measures and reports the share at QUAL 2, 4 and 8.
A winner-to-winner relation needs take n − 1's winner, which depends on n − 2's, and so on: stored state or a long
walk back. I propose not doing that.

**I4 (raising DENS keeps its trigs).** This holds only at QUAL 1. With QUAL > 1, a DENS change can change the winner,
so a fill can jump. Keeping I4 would need the winner chosen independently of DENS (scoring the candidates' key orders,
not their results). That means rendering at a reference density in `best()` and gives up the count term. I propose
accepting the jump and documenting it in `docs/v1/score.md`; QUAL defaults to 1.

## Requests to the lead (`seed_rng.c`, `seed_render.c`)

1. **Mix the candidate only into RHYTHM and PLACE slots** (`slot >> 24` is the layer; `slot_base` would skip `cand`
   for the others). Every candidate then has the same velocities, notes, locks, conditions and timing per step. Three
   things follow:
   - changing ROLE or DENS with QUAL > 1 does not re-roll the steps that keep their trigs;
   - VARY relates the dressing of shown takes exactly, whichever candidate wins;
   - `best()` could run the candidates only through PLACE and the later layers once on the winner (cost about
     q × RHYTHM..PLACE + 1 full chain, in place of q full chains).

   Without this request, the design above still holds; only those three benefits are lost.
2. Agree that QUAL breaks I4 (above), or say which alternative you want.

## Open points

- The two terms and their order are this note's choice. A third term was considered and left out: spread, which
  penalises the largest gap between trigs (BIAS cannot express it). It can be added to the low bits later.
- Files: `lib/score.c`, `lib/test_score.c`, `tools/tests/31-score.sh`, `docs/v1/score.md` (QUAL's text),
  `docs/screens/f11/`, golden recipes with QUAL > 1. The QUAL row in `pages.c` is already behind `seed_have_score`.
