## Decisions taken

1. **The rule is stock's, not Bjorklund's rotation.** Bjorklund's algorithm and the rule above give the same
   necklace, but stock starts it differently (16 / 5: stock `x...x..x..x..x..`, Bjorklund `x..x..x..x..x...`). The
   rule matched every stock pattern read, so EUCLID uses it.
2. **STEPS and the track's length are separate.** A longer track repeats the cycle, a shorter one plays its start.
   With STEPS = the track's length the result is stock's. (Stock's LEN *is* the track's length.)
3. **PULS above STEPS gives every step**, STEPS outside 1..64 is clamped, PULS below 0 is 0. No refusal, as the
   page's PULS goes to 64 whatever STEPS is.
4. **No random numbers.** The seed and VARY do not change the rhythm. YES with no knob turned still takes the next
   seed (V2), which changes only later layers that draw random values.
5. Stock's PL2, RO1, RO2, TRO and OP are not modelled; PLACE ROT rotates the result.

## Firmware findings (for `docs/firmware-notes-os174.md`, lead)

Checked in the emulator on stock OS 1.74, A01 BD:

- **The EUCLIDEAN page's knobs:** A PL1, B PL2, C LEN, D EUC (on / off), E RO1, F RO2, G TRO, H OP. LEN is the
  track's length (+0x2C5), at least 2. The title shows PULSE GEN 1 while PL1 is being turned.
- **The record:** +0x2CA EUC (as noted), +0x2CB PL1, +0x2CC PL2, +0x2CD RO1, +0x2CE RO2, +0x2CF TRO (each 0x3F
  after a new project's boot, no rotation), +0x2D0 OP (0 at boot). +0x2D1: 64 bytes, per step the number of the
  pulse there (0, 1, …) or 0xFF for none: the pattern stock plays, after PL2 and OP are combined. Entries at and past
  LEN keep old values when LEN is lowered. The flag words at +0x000 are not changed by Euclidean mode, and turning
  EUC off leaves the track with its own trigs (none, on a new track).
- **Stock's pattern** for PL1 = p, LEN = n, PL2 0, no rotation: step j has a pulse when (j × p) mod n < p.
- **Turning a stock knob in dtboot:** `--nudge` wakes a sleeping knob with two 8-count turns, which on 1.74 moves a
  stock value by 2, so a single step cannot be made from rest. One 8-count turn followed by N + 1 turns of 4 counts,
  a frame (10M) apart, moves it exactly N steps (`tools/emu.sh`, `emu_sknob`). The mod's own knobs are unaffected (the
  mod reads the encoder itself).

## Open points

- **The title bar shows the seed (`S1`) with SRC = EUCLID**, although the seed does not change the rhythm. The
  design says the title shows TRK or the seed; with EUCLID (and MAP without CHAOS) the seed matters only to later
  layers. For the lead or F10.
- **`22-euclid` takes about 3.5 minutes** (one emulator run per knob setting, 32 settings). The LEN 16 sweep could
  be cut to fewer pulses if the suite's time matters.
- **`props.h`'s random recipes draw only RANDOM as a source**, so `test_props.c` does not cover EUCLID;
  `test_src_euclid.c` runs the same checks with EUCLID forced. The lead may prefer to add EUCLID to `prop_layer`.
