### The rule

```
drift(i) = sum over the octaves of the value noise at step i / sqrt(octaves), scaled to +-DRFT % of utime_hi,
           clamped to +-DRFT % of utime_hi
utime    = utime + drift(i), clamped to the device's range
```

- Octaves: cells every 2, 4, 8, 16, 32 steps, each shorter than the track's length (16 steps: 3 octaves; 64: 5).
- Each octave is value noise: a value -1024..1024 per cell, linearly interpolated between cells. The last cell
  interpolates back to cell 0 over the steps left, so the curve wraps at the length with no jump.
- Each cell's value is its own slot, `SEED_SLOT(SEED_L_TIME, SEED_P_DRIFT, octave * 256 + cell)`: VARY keeps or
  re-rolls cells (P3), and DRFT shares no slot with another knob (P1). The cells are drawn once per render (62 at
  most).
- By step position, on trigs only, as RAND; a track without micro timing (`SEED_F_UTIME`) is left.

## Decisions taken

1. **By step position, not by trig.** The drift is a function of time, so a step's drift does not depend on which
   steps have trigs: DENS, SYNC and MASK change which steps carry it, not its value (tested). TIME runs after
   PLACE, so a trig moved by ROT or SYNC takes the drift of where it lands.
2. **The scale is RAND's.** DRFT 100 % bounds the drift at `utime_hi` (±23 on the Rytm), as RAND 100 % bounds its
   offset; DRFT is shown as a percentage for that reason (the `pages.c` row). The bound is a clamp of the sum, so
   it is reached (tested at 10, 40, 70 and 100 % for 3 to 64 steps). At DRFT 100 % the mean |drift| is 8 (RAND
   100 %: 11). Dividing by sqrt(octaves) instead of the number of octaves keeps 16- and 64-step tracks at about the
   same size.
3. **Equal weight per octave**, as Voss and McCartney's 1/f generator: the long octaves give the wander, the short
   ones the movement on top. Measured over 400 takes at DRFT 100 %: mean |difference| between neighbouring
   steps 4 (16 steps) and 3 (64), against 15 for RAND 100 %.
4. **The curve wraps at the length**, so the loop has no jump (|step 0 − last step| is as small as between
   neighbours; tested).
5. **Each track has its own curve** (the take's salt is the track). A drift shared by the pattern's tracks (the
   band rushing together) would need the curve to leave out the track, which `struct seed_take` does not allow
   from a stage; see open points.
6. **Tracks of 1 or 2 steps** have no octave shorter than the track and are left unchanged.
7. **F7's `seed_noise` is not used**: it takes one seed for the whole curve, so VARY could not keep part of it, and
   F7 is not merged. The noise here is a few lines over `seed_rand_range`.

## Open points

- **For Alan:** decisions 2 (scale: DRFT 100 % = ±23, mean ±8), 3 (equal weight per octave) and 5 (each track
  drifts on its own). Each is a line or two in `timing_drift.c`.
- **A shared drift across tracks** (decision 5): if wanted, `seed_time_drift` would need a take whose salt is the
  pattern, not the track: a contract change (`seed.h`) or a context field. Not needed for version 1 as written.
- **PHRS (F9):** a page renders take n + offset, so with PHRS each page has its own curve (wrapping at the track's
  length, not the page's). Pages of the same letter share the curve; a page boundary between different letters can
  jump. F9 may want DRFT over the whole length from take n.
- **Cost:** 62 `seed_rand` calls at most per render (a 64-step track); with VARY each walks up to 64 takes. Not
  measured on the instruction counter.
