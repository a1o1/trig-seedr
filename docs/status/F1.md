### The meters

A bar is a prime stratification: the meter's groupings of its note value, top first, then BEAT's prime factors,
smallest first.

| METR | Strata | Counted beat | Bar with BEAT b |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4/4 | 2 2 | quarter | 4b |
| 3/4 | 3 | quarter | 3b |
| 6/8 | 2 3 | dotted quarter | 6b |
| 12/16 | 2 2 3 | dotted eighth | 12b |
| 5/8 | 5 | eighth | 5b |
| 7/8 | 7 | eighth | 7b |

Checks (`test_barlow`): 4/4 = 15 0 8 4 12 2 10 6 14 1 9 5 13 3 11 7; 3 × 2 = 5 0 3 1 4 2 (3/4, BEAT 2);
2 × 3 = 5 0 2 4 1 3 (6/8, BEAT 1); 3 × 2 × 2, 2 × 2 × 3, the basic orders of 5 and 7, and 5 × 2, against hmt's
tables.

### Classes and roles

A step's depth is the first stratum whose grid it lies on. The depths give five classes: D the bar's first step,
G a grouping above the counted beat (4/4's half bar), B a counted beat and nothing more (4/4's beats 2 and 4),
O the counted beat's first division, F finer. Barlow's order keeps depths apart (every step of a depth ranks above
every deeper step; `test_every_meter` checks it for all 6 × 16 meters and BEATs), so each class is a run of ranks.

- **METER** is Barlow's order. **KICK** D G O F B, **BACK** B F O G D, **HAT** O D G B F, **PERC** F O B G D,
  Barlow's order within a class (4/4 orders in `docs/v1/meter.md`).
- **The five-level weight** (STRONG, WEAK, OFF, NOT 1, NOTBAR) is the class: D 4, G 3, B 2, O 1, F 0. In 4/4 it
  equals 0.3's `seed_weight` for BEAT 1 and every even BEAT (checked for steps 0..63).
- **Several bars**: every bar has the same ranks; ties between bars go to the bars' own Barlow order (bars rounded
  up to a power of two: 1, 3, 4, 2 of four). Without this, BIAS 100 on 64 steps would fill bar 1 before bar 2 on
  equal steps.

## Decisions taken

1. **BEAT counts the meter's note value** (a quarter in 4/4 and 3/4, an eighth in 6/8, 5/8 and 7/8, a sixteenth in
   12/16), so one rule covers every meter and 4/4 is unchanged. A 6/8 bar with BEAT 4 is 24 steps; 6/8 wants
   BEAT 2 and 12/16 BEAT 1 (the docs say so). The alternative, BEAT per counted beat, would need BEAT to be a
   multiple of 3 in 6/8.
2. **SHAPE = RANK and AMOD = TOP use the METER order**, not ROLE: ROLE is a RHYTHM knob, and a DYNAMICS result that
   depended on it would break I3. `test_shape_rank` checks ROLE has no effect.
3. **SHAPE = RANK** spreads a bar's ranks evenly over 0..255 (16 levels in 4/4, BEAT 4), the same in every bar.
4. **AMOD = TOP** uses `seed_keys` with BIAS 100: the pure rank order, ties to the earlier step; the seed has no
   effect. ACC owns the accent flag of every trig, as AMOD = RAND (0.2's decision 1).
5. **BEAT's factors go smallest first** (BEAT 6 = 2 × 3), so the half beat is the "&", as in 0.3.
6. **4/4 with an odd BEAT** (3, 5, …, 15): the beat's first division now weighs 1 (0.3: 0), because it is the
   generic O class. 286 of 448 steps change level. No test or golden vector used it; the default BEAT 4 is
   unchanged.

## BIAS's response curve

0.2 noted BIAS was steep with five levels. With Barlow's 16 ranks, trigs on the four beats (steps 1, 5, 9, 13) out
of 1,200 at DENS 25, 16 steps, 4/4, 300 seeds (`test_meter`, printed on every run):

| BIAS | −100 | −80 | −60 | −40 | −20 | 0 | 20 | 40 | 60 | 80 | 100 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| on beats (of 1,200) | 0 | 0 | 0 | 21 | 163 | 290 | 420 | 612 | 850 | 1,082 | 1,200 |
| on step 1 (of 300) | 0 | 0 | 0 | 2 | 38 | 83 | 120 | 184 | 265 | 300 | 300 |

With 0.3's five levels (0.2's status): BIAS +60 put 963 on beats, −60 none. The curve is now gentler above 0 and
still reaches every beat at +100. From −60 down no beat gets a trig at DENS 25: the 12 steps off the beats take all
four places first.
