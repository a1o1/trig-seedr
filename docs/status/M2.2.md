### What the host fuzzer checks

| Check | How |
|---|---|
| I1 | the case's recipe with every layer at 0 (V4), the TAKE knobs (SEED, VARY, PHRS, CVAR, QUAL, METR, BEAT) and every other knob kept: byte-identical, and `seed_layer_active` 0 for every layer |
| I2 | rendered twice, the second time with `seed_work` and the output full of other bytes: same result and bytes; trig bits past the other tracks' lengths in the context change nothing; the original, context and recipe are not written |
| I3 | each layer PLACE..FIELDS drawn again (`prop_i3`) changes only its fields; each part of a layer at 0 (DYN's velocity, ACC, LEN, NOTE, each lane, COND, RTRG, TIME's micro timing, GVEL) changes only that part's fields; with another DENS a trig that stays keeps the values drawn for its step (RANGE, NOISE) |
| I4 | one share knob per case (DENS, MAP DENS, SYNC, ACC with RAND or TOP, COND, RTRG, each lane's %) raised in random steps: the steps it chose stay chosen (SYNC: the steps it emptied) |
| I5 | RANDOM's exact count and EUCLID's (STEPS = the length) at the source; SYNC keeps the count; MASK and SKIP only remove |
| I6 | steps at and past the length, the length, fields and defaults unchanged |
| I10 | at VARY 100 take n + 1 is byte-identical to take n (the whole render, PHRS, CVAR, QUAL included); on the slots, random takes (also near the wrap of take numbers), tracks, candidates and slots: VARY 0 keeps none, 100 all, and the share kept per VARY decile is within 3 points of the decile's mean |
| ranges | every value of a take is one the device can store: origin −1 or below the length, swing where it was, velocity 1..127, note, length and retrig codes, micro timing inside the device's range, conditions, each lane inside its parameter's range (wild cases included) |
| refusals | a valid recipe is refused only when CVAR meets a track without conditions |
| lanes | (finding 1) two lanes on one parameter: refused, or the take's two lanes agree |

And that every source, shape, value source, PHRS, CVAR, QUAL, SYNC, MASK, FLD, GRV, LEGATO, TOP and lane 2 were drawn.

## Findings

1. **Two lock lanes on one parameter lose lane 1's locks on the device.** The fuzzer's lanes check (two lanes on one
   parameter must agree in the take) failed in 423 of 4,000 cases. In the emulator, before the fix: LOCK lane 1 at
   100 % on SMP (values 10..20) with lane 2 left on SMP at 0 % (the PARM knob lets lane 2 sit on any parameter) wrote
   **no** sample locks: `seedref` showed a lock on every trig, the device's lane 1 row was all `-`. The device stores
   one lock per parameter and step and `rt_seed_target_lock` takes the later lane's value, here lane 2's, which is
   the original's locks carried along. The same happens with FLD on one of two such lanes. Reduced to
   `test_found_lanes` in `lib/test_fuzz.c`. **Fix:** `seed_lock` (`lib/values.c`) refuses with `SEED_E_ARG` when two
   of the device's lanes share a parameter and either has a % above 0; `seed_place_fields` (`lib/place.c`) refuses FLD
   = LANE1 or LANE2 when the lanes share a parameter. Both lanes on one parameter and neither used is still fine (both
   carry the same original locks). On the device this shows SETTING OUT OF RANGE and writes nothing.

No other failure: no invariant, range or sanitizer failure in the short runs, the 5-minute run or the hour (Results).
Four of the fuzzer's own first checks were wrong and were corrected, not the code: lane values out of range when a knob out of range had moved a lane onto another parameter after the original was made (the original now follows the recipe), SYNC's count with MASK after it (a
moved trig meets the other track at its new step), SYNC's count on PHRS's last, shorter page (the page is a 16-step
phrase and plays its start: design A2), and "MASK and SKIP only remove" with SYNC on (SYNC does not move trigs across
SKIP, so SKIP changes which it moves).

## Decisions taken

1. **Two lanes on one parameter are refused** (finding 1), not merged. The conservative choice: nothing is written and
   the screen says why. Alternatives for Alan below.
2. **props.h uses the Rytm's capability table** (`device/rytm_params.h`, 26 parameters, lane defaults SMP and FRQ)
   instead of 0.5's one-parameter stand-in, so the generators and `seedref` agree. A second and third device are in
   `test_fuzz.c` only.
3. **The reduced test lives in `lib/test_fuzz.c`** (a "found" section run on every `make test`), next to the check that
   found it, rather than in `test_values.c`.
4. **The emulator sample is 6 recipes** (about 5 minutes alone): `FUZZ_SAMPLES` and `FUZZ_SAMPLE_SEED` run more or
   others. Lane 1 stays on the sample slot in the sample (the dump format shows that parameter only); lane 2 is read
   from the lock store for its own parameter.
5. **I9 "plays"** is checked as: the sequencer runs the take for 2 G instructions and stops with BD unchanged. Sound is
   not checked.
