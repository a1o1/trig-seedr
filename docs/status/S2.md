# S2: the Syntakt device layer

State: ready for review
Branch: worktree-s2-device-st (from worktree-s0-2-emu-st; S0.5 and S1 merged in)

## Done

- **`device/syntakt_st.h`**: the struct seed_dev of design section 10 for Syntakt OS 1.42, the counterpart of
  `rytm_ar2.h` (same shape, `ST_HOST` for the host). Read, the settings check (room), write, context, snapshot,
  restore, drop and the change notice. Writes go through stock's step routines (place trig, flag set / clear, NOTE,
  NOT2..NOT4, VEL, LEN, micro timing, condition, retrig) and the lock model's set / clear; the two bytes with no
  known setter (+0x240, +0x280) are written directly with the step's notice. A step moves whole (every lock, its
  chord notes and the bytes lib/ does not know); a step with no origin keeps what its position holds.
- **Its memory**: 13 snapshots of 0x499 + 0x2447 bytes and the tracks' lists, `ST_SEED_STATE_SIZE` (about 140 KB),
  given by the mod with `st_seed_attach` (plan SD2: the native heap on the device). Not attached: every entry
  returns SEED_E_NA.
- **Lock lanes (SD4)**: a track's list is `st_track_params` (device/st_params.h, S0.5): the machine's controls,
  then the pages its track shows. The machine is the mod's to give (`st_seed_set_machine`); until then a track's
  list is the shared pages (FLTR, BASEW on tracks 1..8, AMP, LFO). The device's caps follow the track last asked
  about. A lane's parameter goes to a lock slot by stock's own table (below).
- **Conditions**: lib/'s codes 0..64 (the Rytm's) translate to the Syntakt's 0..99; the Syntakt's own LST, !LST and
  negated A:B read as 65..99, which lib/ never makes, so they come back unchanged.
- **Fields**: NOTE, VEL, LEN, micro timing (−23..+23), condition, locks (S0.5's ST_VOICE_FIELDS; the FX track no
  NOTE / VEL). No retrig, accent or slide: their bytes are copied with the step, never generated.
- **`device/test_syntakt_st.c`**: 13,243 checks against a model of stock's routines (the notes' reset on a placed
  trig and on a step left without one, the length check, the lock matrix with its count word and slot bytes):
  attach, the conditions both ways, the lists, read, identity on every track and length (I1), moves by origin with
  every lock and chord note, lanes (fine part kept), refusals with nothing written (I8), a new trig's stock bits, the
  snapshot (I7), the context with Euclidean mode, and 2,000 fuzz cases (`DEV_FUZZ` for more). Three planted bugs
  (a condition off by one, new trigs losing stock's bits, the restore's lock copy removed) fail it (1,579, 1 and
  1,262 failures).
- **`device/cf_syntakt_st.c`**: `make -C device coldfire`: 12,506 bytes of code, 4 of data, no undefined symbols.
  The Makefile's check ignored `nm -u`'s per-file headers only with one object; it now filters them (checked with a
  planted undefined symbol).
- **`tools/st_s2.sh`** with **`mods/devtest-st`** (a test mod, built by `tools/st_build.sh --core mini`, run in
  `../analog-emu-st`): 10 PASS, about 45 s. Attach from the native heap; snapshot; a known take on A01 track 1
  (every step one later, step 3's NOTE 64 / VEL 90 / micro timing +5 / condition 1:2, an FRQ lock 100 on step 2 and
  none on step 5) reads back with `tools/st_peek.py` exactly as intended; track 2 untouched; restore gives the
  record and the lock store byte for byte.

## Findings

- **A parameter's lock slot** is byte +7 of its entry in stock's parameter table (505 entries of 52 bytes at
  `0x4022d83c`, S0.5): `byte[0x4022d843 + 52 * id]`, 255 for a parameter stock does not lock [found by a search
  over the table for the measured pairs; then every other measurement agreed]. Measured in analog-emu (T1 held,
  each knob turned): SYN A..H on BD MODERN → slots 18, 21, 23, 25, 19, 20, 22, 26; FLTR A..H → 32, 33, 34, 35, 28,
  29, 27, 30; AMP → 40, 41, 42, —, 45, 46, 47, 48; LFO A..E → 1..5. Every machine's SYN controls share slots 18..26.
- **The lock routines** are model-level (no length check); the view-level set (`0x400387b4`, Synfact) also adds a
  lock trig on a step without a trig. The layer locks steps below the length only, and adds no trig.
- **The step notice**: each setter writes its byte and calls the track's vtable +0x10 (`0x4002bb20`, which jumps to
  `0x4017b04e`, the model's observer dispatch) with `{0x401c6b9c, step}` [read in the disassembly].
- **`-O3` and memcpy**: elekloader's Syntakt flags (`-O3`, no `-fno-tree-loop-distribute-patterns`) turn copy loops
  and struct copies into calls to `memcpy`, which no mod exports: the link is refused. `mods/devtest-st` adds that
  flag and copies by hand; the seedr mod (S3) needs the same or a `memcpy` of its own.
- **Synfact's "sequencer copy"** (`0x43bd7430`, `chord.h`) does not hold the record's layout at boot in analog-emu.
  A copy of the first 0x100 bytes (flags, VEL, LEN) is at `0x43c0e1c4`; it does not follow a write and matches again
  after the restore, which looks like the pattern's saved copy (RELOAD), not what the sequencer plays [measured;
  meaning inferred].

## Open points

- **A track's machine type** is not found. A memory diff over a machine change (BD MODERN → SD BASIC with FUNC + SYN)
  gave candidates (`0x43bdd4db` and others) without a Sound layout around them. Synfact reads a Sound through the
  TRIG view's parameter proxy (`view vtable +0xc4 (view, 0x60, -1)`, `mods/arp-st/view.c`), for the current track
  only. Until S3 finds it, lanes offer the shared pages; the SYN page's controls need it.
- **Playback**: whether the sequencer plays a take the layer writes (the setters' notices) is not checked: S4, by
  the DSP's output or the trig LEDs during PLAY.
- **Retrig**: its encoding is not measured, so retrig is not a field (bytes copied with the step).
- **+0x240 / +0x280** are written directly (no known setter); +0x240's two setters (`0x40028076`, `0x4002810e`) are
  not identified.
- Steps cleared by stock when left without a trig: the model assumes their locks are cleared too, as on the Rytm;
  the layer writes locks last, so either way the result is the target.
- The routines' addresses are inferred from the disassembly (firmware notes section 4); the emulator check covers
  place trig, NOTE, VEL, micro timing, condition, the lock set and clear and the restore, not retrig or flag bits
  other than the trig.
- The analog-emu build used (`../analog-emu-st`, local branch `syntakt-modified`) is S0.3's, unchanged and not
  pushed.
