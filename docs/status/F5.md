### The rule

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| grid step | RHYTHM's GRID with SOURCE = RANDOM (1, 2, 4, 8); 1 for every other source |
| weaker | a lower `seed_rank(METR, BEAT, ROLE_METER, step, length)` (F1) |
| candidate | a trig at or past SKIP; the step one grid step before it (round the loop) is empty, at or past SKIP, weaker, and no trig lies between them |
| run | two or more consecutive trigs (step order, round the loop, from SKIP) all moved |
| resolves | the trig after a run stays and is stronger than every step the run left |

Candidates are tried in the order of the take's random keys (`seed_keys`, slot `SEED_P_SYNC`, P1); a move is kept
when every run still resolves. SYNC % takes `seed_share` of the kept moves, in that order (P2). A move swaps the
trig's whole step with the empty one (origin, lanes, every field, unknown flag bits); swing stays with the position.

## Decisions taken

1. **"Runs resolve onto a strong step"** is read as above: a lone anticipation is always allowed (an onset on the
   weaker step and a rest on the stronger one is the syncopation); two or more consecutive anticipations must end
   before a trig that stays and is stronger than every step they left. This also rules out moving every trig,
   which would only rotate the track. A first version required every moved trig to resolve onto a stronger trig;
   it left dense RANDOM takes, GRID 2 and the 6/8 examples untouched at SYNC 100 %, so it was dropped.
2. **The grid step is RANDOM's GRID, 1 otherwise.** The design says "one grid step"; only RANDOM has a grid.
   SOURCE = TRACK, EUCLID, MAP and COPY anticipate by one step.
3. **Weaker uses ROLE = METER**, whatever ROLE says, as SHAPE = RANK and AMOD = TOP do (F1's decision 2): ROLE is
   a RHYTHM knob, and PLACE must not depend on it.
4. **SKIP:** trigs on the steps SKIP clears are not counted (they are going), and no trig moves into those steps.
   So SYNC with SKIP has as many trigs as SKIP alone. A consequence: SKIP can change which trigs SYNC moves near
   its edge (a trig on step SKIP + 1 cannot be anticipated), so SKIP N with SYNC is not "SYNC, then the first N
   steps cleared". `test_props.c`'s SKIP check (0.2) compares whole renders with and without SKIP; it does not
   turn SYNC on, so it is unaffected.
5. **A trig is never moved past another** (GRID > 1, after ROT on a length that is not a multiple of GRID).
6. **Moves swap the two steps**, so the empty step's own data (trigless locks, unknown flags) is kept, at the
   trig's old place, as ROT keeps it.
7. **The share is of the kept moves**, not of the candidates: SYNC 100 % makes every move the rule allows.

## Findings

### 1. m68k-elf-gcc 16.2 miscompiles a step swap (store merging)

The first build passed every host test and failed on the device: SYNC's take was refused with SETTING OUT OF RANGE
(the device's write rejected it). The take in the mod's memory had lost the moved trig, and the step before it
held 4 bytes of another step. The swap

```c
tmp = t->s[p]; t->s[p] = t->s[s]; t->s[s] = tmp;
t->s[p].flags = (t->s[p].flags & ~SEED_SWING) | sw_p;   /* swing stays */
t->s[s].flags = (t->s[s].flags & ~SEED_SWING) | sw_s;
```

compiles at `-mcpu=54455 -O2` (elekloader's Rytm flags, also `make -C lib coldfire`'s) to a `movel %a5@,%a0@` whose
destination is `&t->s[p]` − 6 (the step array's offset in `struct seed_track`): the first 4 bytes of the copy
(flags, note, velocity) go to the wrong place. The other stores of the copy are right. It reproduces in a
20-line file (the function above over `lib/seed.h`'s types), at -O2 and -O3. `-fno-store-merging` and
`-fno-tree-sra` make it go away; the same swap without the flag fix-up compiles correctly.

`place_sync.c` now sets swing in the two copies and stores each step whole (checked in the disassembly and in
the emulator). With `-fno-store-merging`, 24 functions in `lib/` and `pages.c` (`seed_place_sync` itself among
them) compile differently (`seed_rotate`,
`seed_place_rot`, `seed_track_copy`, `chain`, `seed_trig`, `pg_*`, ...); store merging is a common optimisation,
so that alone does not mean they are wrong, and the emulator tests of ROT and the pages pass. Not checked
one by one.

### 2. The device layer does not find the rows of locks made by hand

In the build with no lock rows (`RT_ROWS_MAX 0`) SYNC moving a step with three FLTR locks made by hand (sound
indexes 27, 30, 31) is refused, also after SAVE PATTERN. Instrumented (a temporary `RT_DBG` record in
`rt_seed_room_src`, not committed): stock makes BD's rows (type, track) = (18, 0), (20, 0), (21, 0) when the locks
are set (they are in the row table before any save), while `RT_LOCK_TYPE(k)` (the table at 0x401c5be4, as int32 by
sound index) gives 36, 1 and 6 for 27, 30 and 31. So `have` is never true for these locks, and every lock a write
moves or keeps on another step counts as a new row.

Effects: with `RT_ROWS_MAX` 71 nothing shows until a pattern has about 69 rows in use; then an origin move (SYNC,
ROT on SOURCE = TRACK) of a step with locks would be refused although stock needs no new row. The opposite error
(a new lock accepted because a wrong type happens to have a row, then lost at the save) may also be possible; not
tested. `25-sync.sh` checks what the plan asks (refused when no row fits, nothing written) and does not depend on
this.

## How to test

```sh
make -C lib test                 # test_place_sync: worked cases, properties, I2 I3 I4 I5 I6 with SYNC on
make -C lib coldfire
sh tools/golden.sh               # 60 same (49 before F5, 11 sync-*), 1 pending
sh tools/run_tests.sh 25         # the emulator test, about 2.5 minutes
sh tools/run_tests.sh            # everything (gate rule 2)
```

`test_place_sync` (27 checks):

- worked cases in 4/4: one move with its whole step and swing; four on the floor (over 200 seeds every result is
  one of the four the rule allows, each one occurs, never all four moved); runs that would end on a weak trig;
  a lone trig; SKIP 4 and SKIP 1; METR out of range refused;
- 3,000 random tracks and recipes (every 0.2 layer, METR, BEAT, GRID, ROT, SKIP, SOURCE = RANDOM or TRACK):
  the count of trigs is unchanged (I5); every moved trig went exactly one grid step earlier onto a weaker, empty
  step at or past SKIP, past no other trig; no trig appears from nowhere; every run resolves;
- I4: raising SYNC keeps every move (vacated steps nest), 500 tracks;
- SOURCE = TRACK: every step is its origin's step, swing aside (whole-step moves, lanes included);
- I2, I6 and I3 for DYNAMICS .. FIELDS through `props.h`, with SYNC on;
- P1: another seed gives another take on 130 of 200 tracks at SYNC 50.

It prints `SYNC: 2819 moves in 3000 takes (GRID > 1: 238 takes, SKIP: 574)`.

A mutation check (scratch script, not committed) broke each rule in turn: no run rule, every trig may move, SKIP
ignored, not weaker, moved past a trig, a refused move kept. Each makes `test_place_sync` fail.

`25-sync.sh` (16 checks): see the file's header. Part 2 makes the locked step by hand as `11-device.sh` does (GRID
REC, T5 held, three FLTR knobs), then checks the 42 lock words of steps 4 and 5 directly in the lock store.

## Open points

- **For Alan / the lead:** decisions 1 (the reading of "runs resolve onto a strong step"), 2 (grid step) and 4
  (SKIP near its edge). Each is a few lines in `place_sync.c`.
- **MASK (F4)** runs after SYNC and only removes trigs; it can remove the trig a run resolves onto. MASK's
  guarantee is on the result (P5), so this is by design; F4 or the lead may want SYNC to treat steps MASK will
  clear as SKIP does.
