# S0.4: Syntakt OS 1.42 firmware notes

State: ready for review
Branch: worktree-s0-4-notes (from worktree-s0-2-emu-st)

## Done

- **`docs/firmware-notes-st142.md`**: getting the app, pattern and track; the 0x499-byte track record; the trig
  condition codes (0..99, differing from the Rytm's from 30); the stock step, flag, trig and lock routines; the lock
  store (a full matrix: 64 steps × 72 int16 per track, no row pool); grid recording; the TRIG view and its
  sub-page; the Euclidean fields; the working-state save. Each finding marked measured or inferred; Synfact's
  findings credited.
- **`tools/st_peek.py`**: a snapshot read as trig-seedr needs it: `dump` (a track in `tools/emu.py dump`'s format,
  `--lane ID` for a lock id), `rec` (grid recording), `trigpage` (the TRIG sub-page, −1 when not shown), `euc`.
- **`tools/st_s04.sh`**: 13 PASS, about 70 s. Boot: track 1's length 64 and trigs; RECORD: grid recording 1; T5: a
  new trig with its reset fields; T1 held on TRIG, A and B turned: step 1's NOTE (+0x2c0) and VEL (+0x080); T1 held
  on SYN, A turned: a lock of id 18 on step 1; TRIG, TRIG: sub-page 0 then 1; knob D: EUC on.
- Capstone in a venv under `build/` (`build/venv`); the unpacked OS under `build/st-fw/`. Neither is committed.

## Measured vs inferred

- **Measured:** the app pointer; pattern A01's track records, lock store and settings; flag bit 0x0001; VEL and
  NOTE offsets; a new trig's field values; a lock's place and the step count word; the grid-recording bytes; the
  TRIG view's sub-page; EUC, PL1 and the Euclidean table; the track length word in per-track mode.
- **Inferred (disassembly):** the other field offsets (LEN, micro timing, retrig, condition) from their setters;
  the condition table; the flag set, trig place, step reset and lock set / clear routines; no lock-row limit; the
  COKI working-state writer.
- **Unknown:** retrig encodings; +0x240 / +0x280; the whole-step shift; the lock value scale per id; the power-off
  trigger in analog-emu.

## Open points

- After the held-trig edits in `st_s04.sh`, knob D on the Euclidean page did not turn EUC on; from a new boot it
  does. Not looked into (the script reboots for that part).
- Capstone does not decode ColdFire's `mvs` / `mvz`; read them by hand (`0111 rrr 1zs eee`: z=0 mvs, z=1 mvz,
  s=0 byte, s=1 word).
