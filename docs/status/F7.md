### The noise

One octave of value noise. Corner c sits on step c × ZOOM and has a random level 0..255; between two corners
the level follows a smoothstep, 3t² − 2t³ with t in 1/256 of a cell, all in integers (the largest product,
t² × (768 − 2t), is below 2^26). ZOOM 1 is a random level per step; ZOOM is clamped to 1..64. ColdFire: `noise.c` 747
bytes of text, `dyn_noise.c` 36, no data or `.bss`.

## Decisions taken

1. **Each corner is its own slot** (P1, P3). In the chain, corner c's level is `seed_rand(take, slot + c)`: the
   NOISE value source uses `slot + 0x200 + c` (the base `seed.h` gives it), SHAPE = NOISE `SEED_SLOT(DYN,
   SEED_P_SHAPE, c)`. So VARY k % keeps about k % of the corners from take n to n + 1, and the contour varies
   partly, as the design's "related takes" asks. One seed per take from `slot + 0x200` (a literal reading of
   `seed.h`'s sentence) would have kept or replaced the whole contour at once. See request 1.
2. **A trig's value is its step's**, not its place among the trigs (`index`). DENS, the lock %, SKIP and MASK do
   not change the value of a trig that stays (tested), and raising a lane's % keeps every lock with its value (I4).
3. **Range mapping in equal shares**: `lo + level × (hi − lo + 1) / 256`. At ZOOM 1 each value of the range is as
   likely as with RANGE (tested: 1..8 within ±10 % over 32,000 draws). Level 0 gives `lo`, 255 gives `hi`, so a
   range given high to low turns the contour over (`v(hi, lo) = lo + hi − v(lo, hi)`, tested).
4. **Step 0 is a corner.** *Superseded after the merge: the contour now wraps at the track's length (Alan; the lead added `length` to `struct seed_take` and changed `seed_noise_take`; 7 noise vectors rewritten).* Originally the contour did not wrap at the track's length: `seed_value_noise` and
   `seed_shape_noise` are not given the length. See open point 1.
5. **`seed_noise(seed, x, zoom)`**, the contract's pure function, uses the same curve with corners
   `hash(seed ^ hash(c))`; x may be negative (floor division). The chain does not call it (decision 1); it is there
   for a later user (F8's drift may want it).
6. **SHAPE = NOISE uses N as its zoom** (design section 5), clamped to 1..64 as EVRY clamps N.

`test_noise` (434 checks):

- the noise: 0..255; every ZOOM-th step is its corner's level; every step lies between its two corners; one step
  changes by at most smoothstep's steepest slope; a quarter cell in is 40/256 of the way (smoothstep, not a line);
  ZOOM 1 is the corners; ZOOM clamped; negative x; another seed another contour;
- smoothness: mean change per step ×100 at ZOOM 1, 4, 16: 8560, 2160, 552;
- the range: bounds, either order (mirror), lo = hi constant, equal shares at ZOOM 1;
- VARY (P3, I10): corners kept from take n to n + 1 at VARY 0, 50, 80, 100: 0 %, 49 %, 79 %, 100 %;
- NOTE, both lanes (clamped to the parameter) and SHAPE against `seed_value_noise` / `seed_shape_noise` after a
  full render; DENS 40 → 80 keeps notes and lengths of trigs in both; lane % 30 → 70 keeps every lock's value; N
  changes the shape; VARY 100 gives the same take for two seeds;
- P1: NOTE, lane 1, lane 2 and SHAPE draw different contours from one take;
- I1, I2, I6 and I3 (every layer) on 1,500 random tracks and recipes (`props.h`) with NOISE on in NOTE, lane 1
  or SHAPE: no failures, no refusals.

The tests were checked against four deliberate bugs, one at a time: corners not from the take's slots (VARY
ignored), the range mapped by rounding (ends half as likely), a straight line in place of the smoothstep, and the
`0x200` offset removed. The first three make tests fail. The last does not and cannot: a field uses one source at a
time, so NOISE's slots never meet RANGE's or LIST's in one render; the offset only keeps the convention `seed.h`
states.

`27-noise.sh` (8 checks after the kept2 fixture's 9): the recipe's ranges written into the store, then F7's knobs
turned by hand: NSRC = NOISE with ZOOM 3; LOCK lane 1 at 100 % with SRC = NOISE, ZOOM 8; SHAPE = NOISE, AMT 100, N 6;
each take equal to seedref's; NO; VARY 100 makes seed 3 the same take as seed 2; kept, SAVE + RELOAD PATTERN.

## Open points

1. **The contour does not wrap round the loop.** With a length that is not a multiple of ZOOM, or a long ZOOM, the
   step after the last one (step 1 again) can be far from it. A contour that closes on itself needs the track's
   length in `seed_value_noise` and `seed_shape_noise` (a contract change: an argument, or the length in
   `struct seed_take`). For Alan: is a seam at the loop point acceptable for version 1?
2. **Cost**: a NOISE value draws two slots per step (the corners either side) where RANGE draws one; with VARY
   above 0 each draw walks back up to 64 takes. Not measured on the instruction counter (M2's performance task).
