## Decisions taken

0. **The title bar and VIEW's footer** (Alan, 2026-11-15, after trying 0.4 in the window): the page marks along the
   title's foot read as a broken line, so the title shows the page's place as text (`VIEW BD 9/10`), left out when a
   long title would not fit. VIEW has 0.3's divider and "Trig Seedr" footer again in place of the strip, which
   repeated its steps; the other pages keep the strip (the preview, F10).

1. **0.3 and 0.4 were merged on an integration branch**, not straight onto `main`: the mod they change could not pass
   release 0.3's emulator checks until those were restated (this task).
2. **`lib/groove.h` stays as a shim** rather than being deleted, so Alan's local `grooves-live.h` builds without
   being made again from his Live grooves.
3. **`device/rytm_params.h`** was added (a split of 0.4's header) so seedref renders with the device's own lanes.
   Before, seedref had a one-parameter table and lane 1's default index differed from the mod's.
4. **`tools/build_release.sh` uses the harness's build.** It adds `-std=gnu17` to core-ar2 on gcc 15 or later, as the
   checks do (0.5's decision 2), so a release can be built on the Mac too. Releases are still made in the Linux shell
   (`dev-environment.md`).
5. **The r03 profile is removed** from `tools/emu.sh`: release 0.3's mod is no longer in the tree to test.
6. **Helpers kept:** `lib/tests.h` (0.2) and `lib/props.h` (0.5) both stay. They overlap a little (random tracks), and
   merging them would mean editing six of 0.2's test files for no change in coverage.

## Found during integration

- **I3 and unknown flag bits** (fixed on `main` before this branch, `f68a30a`): ROT moves the flag bits lib/ does not
  know with their steps, as 0.3's ROTATE did. `props.h` counted them with swing as fields no layer may change.
- **A recipe made on an empty track stays RAND.** 0.3's test recorded trigs with seedr open on an empty BD, then
  pressed YES: with RANDOM now built, BD's recipe (made when seedr first showed the empty track, V6) rendered a RANDOM
  take over the recorded trigs. This follows V6 as written. `10-pages` now sets SRC = TRACK by hand. See open points.
- **`emu_recipe` wrote only the named fields** over a store of zeros (NOTE 0..0 and LEN 0..0 then counted as set).
  It now writes a whole recipe (`seedref --recipe`).
- `device/test_rytm_ar2` (a built binary) was committed by mistake in `fd30c0a` and removed in `848256f`; `.gitignore`
  covers it now.
